## The claims

1. **Ep 13 adapts ch 26 pp 1-11 (the Water-shroom operation, Nanachi joining, the departure) — SOUND, and the mid-chapter stop has an independent witness the verdict did not name.** The fandom Episode_13 page's last two refs are `{{Ref|m|026|1-7}}` ("Back at the hut, Nanachi and Reg remove the Water-shroom from Riko's arm … Riko asks Nanachi to come with them … Nanachi agrees") and `{{Ref|m|026|8-11}}` ("prepare their things to leave, and Nanachi looks back at her hut for the last time before they depart"). Chapter_026's own synopsis is two paragraphs: (1) the operation, the scar, the thumb, Riko's invitation, Nanachi agrees; (2) "After extensive preparations … the trio leaves Nanachi's Hideout, reaching the Garden of the Flowers of Fortitude … Reg spots a Delver … Nanachi recognizes the Delver as an Umbra Hand." The INDEPENDENT witness that S1 stops before paragraph (2): the fandom Movie_3 (Dawn of the Deep Soul) page opens its synopsis with "The group embarks from Nanachi's Hideout, reaching the Garden of the Flowers of Fortitude. There they encounter one of Bondrewd's Umbra Hands.{{Ref|m|026}}" and lists ch 26 first in its Adapted From — i.e. the film re-adapts the departure and is the first animation of the garden / Umbra Hand tail. Two reviews written on air date describe the finale's closing beat as the balloon's ascent then credits (starcrossedanime: "as I saw the small balloon float through the various beautiful levels of the abyss … Suddenly roll credits"; crowsworldofanime ends on Nanachi agreeing to join), neither mentions a flower field, Lyza's grave, an Umbra Hand or the Blaze Reap. AFG prints S1 = "manga 1 to 26" AND Movie 3 = "chapters 26 to 38" — the overlap is the straddle, as the verdict says. So END = 26 on fandom (page-level) + AFG + the film's own opening; MU's "Vol 4, Chap 26 (Movie 3)" start is a fourth, weaker witness (a boundary convention, not a content claim). END high is earned.
