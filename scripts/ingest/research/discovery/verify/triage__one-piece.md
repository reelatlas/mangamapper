## Findings, ranked by damage

### 1. 3D2Y's `chapters` string carries arc-wide ranges that are not the pages the special redraws — they must be narrowed before anything lands as `chapters_adapted` (medium-low — nothing renders from a triage row, but this is exactly the string a per-work author would copy)

The beats the two sources actually name are three: (a) Luffy reaching the scaffold → Akainu's attack → Ace's death — the last few chapters of the arc (the death is ch 574, confirmed on the `Chapter 574` page: "Portgas D. Ace Dies … Ace hugs Luffy while taking his last breath"), not 550-580; (b) one childhood scene of Luffy and Ace "vowing to get stronger and go sail the seas" (wiki line 37; no Sabo in the cast list for that scene) — a single Post-War-Arc beat, not 581-597; (c) the reunion and departure, chs 598-602 (this one is tight). ja-WP's phrasing 冒頭のマリンフォード頂上戦争・ルフィと仲間達とのエピソード・終盤の…再集結する場面 names the same three.
- The verdict is honest that "no source prints chapter numbers for the special" (the 3D2Y infobox has no `chapter` field, unlike EoL's) — the arc ranges are the author's framing. Carried into a per-work `chapters_adapted: [550-580, 581-597, 598-602]` they would over-claim by roughly forty chapters of a work whose own licensor calls it 完全新作.
- What would settle it: the per-work author's page check of the prologue against the end of the Marineford arc (≈ the five chapters ending at 574) and the childhood beat against the ASL chapters, then a `chapters_adapted` with two or three short spans and a `recap`/graft-style note. No triage value moves.

### 2. The summary's closing sentence contradicts the EoL row's own page fact — and the fact is verified (low-medium — a ruling-facing summary sentence; the row is right)

- Summary, last sentence: "Nothing on this shelf adapts a chapter the TV run has not already covered." EoL row: the wiki says the origin of Luffy's under-eye scar "is animated here 'for the first time in the anime'". The summary hedges this earlier as "the one possible exception" and then states the categorical.
- Verified on a second page of the same wiki: `Episode 4` Anime Notes, line 73 — "In the manga, Luffy cut his face and created a scar under his left eye to prove to Shanks he is a real man but this is not shown in this episode." `Chapter 1` line 22 places the scene at the chapter's opening (Luffy "takes a knife and stabs himself below his left eye" aboard the Red Hair Pirates' ship). So at PAGE level EoL carries ch 1 material the TV run had not animated (until Episode of East Blue, 2017); at CHAPTER level the summary's sentence is true because ep 4 adapts ch 1. Both wiki statements are one source family; ja-WP's EoL article does not mention the scar.
- Why it matters: it is the fact most likely to move EoL's T28 call (the flashbacks are not pure duplicates of eps 4 / 1). Fix at curation: qualify the sentence ("…has not already covered, apart from one ch 1 page in Episode of Luffy") or drop it. What would settle it independently: a second source family (ja-WP's episode list or the Episode of East Blue article) on the scar's first animation — the per-work verdict's job.
