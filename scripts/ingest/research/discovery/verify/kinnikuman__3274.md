The consistent form — and the one two external records already print — is **`Kinnikuman: Ooabare! Seigi Choujin`**. The verdict's own fallback (`Segi → Seigi` only) would leave the first misreading standing.
- Landing: `validate-extras --entries` does NOT carry `repairs` — the `title:` field of the RelatedWorkRuling must be added by hand (the type's P8 comment: a ruled title beats AniList's staging on every run).
- What would settle it: nothing further — MAL, Toei and ja-WP agree on the reading.
