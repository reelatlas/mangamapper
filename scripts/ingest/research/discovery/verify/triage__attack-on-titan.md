## Findings, ranked by damage

### 1. Flag 3 ("ONE WORK, FOUR ENTRIES … Segments #26-#37 (the Season 2 block) are held by neither of these ids … worth a completeness check") — the premise is a misreading; there is no missing block (medium-low: a flag to Sean, nothing renders, but it sends someone hunting for a fifth AniList entry that does not exist)

- The verdict reads the S3 / Final labels as a running segment count continuing S1's 25 ("19391 (S1, segments #1-25)"). They are not. The S1 shorts are titled 01日目〜25日目 ("Day N"); the "#N" scheme begins with S3 and **#N is the number of the TV episode on that disc**. The alignment is exact on every volume: S3 vol 1 holds eps 38-40 and carries #38 / #39 / #40; vol 4 holds eps 47-49 → #47-#49; vol 7 is the four-episode volume (4話収録 / 本編約96分, eps 56-59) and carries **four** segments #56-#59; Final vol 1 holds eps 60-67 and carries 8 stories, vol 2 eps 68-75 → 8 stories. One parody per episode on the disc.
- Season 2's discs carried no chibi theatre at all. Licensor S2 vol 1 (`season2/product/bd_1.php`, 2017-06-21): 映像特典 = ノンクレジットOP / ノンクレジットED. Vol 2 (`bd_2.php`, 2017-08-18): 映像特典 = 第1PV / 第2PV. ja-WP's 映像特典 section for S2 lists only VR works (vol 1: 進撃の巨人展360°体感シアター“哮”; vol 2: リヴァイ編 / エレン&ミカサ編 VRCG). ja-WP's own S3 title 『ちみキャラ劇場 4コマ!調査兵団リヴァイ班』 says the same thing: the S3 run is a new 4コマ series, not a continuation.
- Settles it: the completeness check is answered — four entries, four disc runs (S1, S3 Part 1, S3 Part 2, Final Part 1), nothing between. The flag should be rewritten so the sitting does not open a search. (The summary's "the same continuing chibi-theatre series" is fine as a description of the format; the "segments #26-#37" arithmetic is what is wrong.)

- Worth one line in the ruling note to close the door on the "picture drama" name: the only drama-shaped extras on S1 are audio or live — vol 2's 諫山創監修ニセ予告ドラマCD 『サシャの怒濤の料理バトル編！！』 (audio), and vol 9's 特典DISC recording of the 2013-10-12 Reading & Live event 「Attack 音 体感」 (a 朗読劇 — a staged reading, ~120 min).
