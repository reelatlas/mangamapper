## Findings, ranked by damage

### 1. The standing's stated BASIS is a stretched precedent and its stated FALLBACK is the wrong branch (medium-low — ruling-facing; nothing renders differently, `canon` holds under every defensible reading)

- The verdict hangs `canon` on "the GTO rule (a side story serialized inside the source IS the source)". That line is real — the research-agent definition, the Toroko Oppai Scenes — but its facts are: serialized in GTO's OWN run (Weekly Shonen Magazine) and collected in GTO's volumes. Darkness is neither: Shueisha's own copy says 「本誌では読めない」 (a 増刊, not the run), and it is コミックス未収録 (Abema; ynjn lists both as standalone 特別読切 titles; ja-WP's 書誌 never mentions it; the fandom pages carry no Volume field). The summary's "serialized inside the source" is therefore not literally true. The honest basis is the one the verdict's own evidence already proves: the PUBLISHER files it as 「かぐや様は告らせたい〜天才たちの恋愛頭脳戦〜」の番外編 / 外伝 under the series' own title, by the series' author, and bundles its adaptation with the series' own volume 22 (「第22巻アニメOVA同梱版には、なんとダークネスも収録！」); the owner's wiki files it as "a special chapter of the … manga series"; T28's text counts "an omake or side story that IS the plot"; N35's Dai clause delivers unnumbered extras as side_story facts. Say that, not "the GTO rule".
- The fallback is mis-stated. Flag 2: "If Sean rules the other way — Darkness as a distinct work — the standing becomes mixed". Under T26 as ruled on 2026-09-05, a distinct work drawn BY THE SOURCE'S AUTHOR HIMSELF is the act shape the sitting accepted ("Kishimoto drawing the boruto-18 omake is the act" → `adopted{author}`), not the Kodachi / illustrator shape it rejected — so a distinct-work ruling lands `canon` + `adopted{author}` (the PER WORK `adopted` field, "a note for the sitting"), and `mixed` appears only if Sean rules it distinct AND declines adoption. T26 also says "the author's own OTHER MANGA is never this question: it is a chain question (I41)" — but I41's chain is continuous-story works, which a pair of gag 読切 is not; the vocabulary has no exact slot for an author's own uncollected extra, which is precisely why this is Sean's call and why the verdict was right to flag it. Two of three readings give canon; the verdict's standing survives all of them.
- What would settle it: Sean's ruling on whether an author-drawn 番外編 printed in the publisher's 増刊 and never collected is "the instrument" (the Dai / GTO shape) or "the author's other work" (the boruto-18 shape). The evidence is complete either way; no further fetch changes it.
