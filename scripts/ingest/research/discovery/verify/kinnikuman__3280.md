## Findings, ranked by damage

### 1. The licensor leg is stronger than the verdict cites — Toei names the special on Vol.5, ahead of ep 48 — and that settles the precedent question in the same direction (medium: it changes the BASIS of a value that will render, not the value)

- The verdict rests the DVD leg on Toei Video's catalogue pages and an inference: Vol.4 = 271 min / 11 eps, Vol.5 = 295 min / 9 eps, "every one of the twelve volumes runs 24.50-24.67 min per episode except Vol.5 at 32.78, an excess of 73 minutes that is this special". Reproduced on all twelve pages, fetched today: Vol.1 294 / 12 = 24.50; Vols 2, 3, 6, 7, 10, 12 = 296 / 12 = 24.67; Vols 4, 8, 9, 11 = 271 / 11 = 24.64; Vol.5 = 295 / 9 = 32.78. But the catalogue pages never NAME the special — 収録話 reads 「第48話 … ～第56話まで収録」 and 映像特典 is 超人データファイル — so as cited the leg is arithmetic plus a pixiv cross-check.
- Not cited, and it states the fact: Toei Video's 特集 page `https://www.toei-video.co.jp/special/kinniku/` (fetched raw; the string is in the page body and in its `og:description`): 「VOL.４ 第37話～第47話 ／ VOL.５ TVスペシャル＋第48話～第56話 ／ VOL.６ 第57話～第68話」. The 1983 series has exactly one TV special, so 「TVスペシャル」 is unambiguous. The licensor puts the special on the volume that opens the Devil Chojin arc and lists it AHEAD of ep 48.
- For Sean's precedent question — does a licensor's disc placement qualify as a licensor ORDER the way AoT's #3.5 did? Partly, and the difference should be stated rather than blurred. AoT's licensor printed ordinals that NAME the slot (#3.25 / #3.5 / #3.75 between TV eps 3 and 4). Toei prints a listing order — special, then 48-56 — which is consistent with 47.5 and inconsistent with 51.5 (a disc of 48-56 with the special listed first cannot mean "between 51 and 52"), but does not by itself distinguish "watch it before 48" from "it ships in this box". So the ruling basis is what the protocol itself names: the SETTING — ja-WP, verbatim, 「原作での時間軸は第21回超人オリンピック前だったが、本作はオリンピック後の設定になっている」, the protocol's own "a film set between S1 and S2 sits after the S1 finale even if it aired during S2" case — plus the wiki's stated viewing order (the Anime Timeline page and the prev = 47B / next = 48A chain on all three pages) and the mid-match defect of 51.5 (our own ep-51 anchor note: 51A opens the Black Hole match at ch 127; ja-WP's 52 and 53 titles close it). The licensor's listing corroborates; it is not the anchor. Both readings give 47.5.
- The verdict's escape hatch ("if the halo field is meant to record the broadcast slot, 51.5 is correct") is moot: the halo field is already the watch slot by precedent — AoT 25.51 → 3.5 and KonoSuba 20.5 → 20.52 were both ruled OFF the air-date derivation, and the protocol defines the field as "after which episode a viewer should watch it". A broadcast-order definition would contradict two standing rulings. Recommend CORRECTED to 47.5, no alternative offered.

## The T28 question, decided on evidence

Is `mixed` right, or is the adapted spine so dominant that canon-with-divergence is the better reading? Mixed. T28's faithfulness clause keeps a row canon through "alterations, cuts, reorders and omissions of the source's OWN events" — and the special's first act is exactly that: the recruitment order inverted, Ingen recast, two of the seven swapped for Robin Mask, Warsman and Rikishiman, four invented test-duels (the last already an original insertion, but inside an adapted act whose job — assemble the seven and get them to Rakka — is ch 86-87's job). The second half is not an alteration of ch 88-89's events; it is a different plot: a seven-ring 団体戦 with seven named opponents and seven results, a leader the manga does not have, an abduction and a forced wedding, a Kinniku Buster finish, the Prince Medaka reveal and a marriage, Ramenman staying behind — "a NEWLY ANIMATED sequence of significant length … carrying scenes the source lacks" (T28's recap boundary), which "weighs toward filler, and with a real canon share in the same episode the row is mixed". The canon share is real and substantial: the arc's premise and assembly, the bandits' two defining gimmicks (they break off to eat — ch 88; they split into copies — ch 89), the heart as the weak point, and the outcome — and it is the SOLE animation of those chapters anywhere in the catalogue, which the sole-adaptation exception would flip to mixed even at a smaller share. Filler is excluded outright (overlap never proves filler; the chapters' beats are in it); canon is excluded because the climax is replaced, not retold. Sean has ruled the shape twice on this programme — Movie 2 on this same owner (the Bibimba act beside the Black Emperor half) and The First Slam Dunk (the Sannoh game beside the Ryota thread) — and once on episodes (the batch 21 wave 2 seam-episode standing). `mixed`, `high`: ja-WP + the EN wiki + Kayama name the same chapters and the same originals; the share split is a beat count, as the verdict says, and a stopwatch would only move the fraction, never the standing.

## The position question, stated plainly for Sean

- The protocol defines the field as the WATCH slot ("after which episode a viewer should watch it"; "a film set between S1 and S2 sits after the S1 finale even if it aired during S2"), and two rulings already apply it that way (AoT 3.5, KonoSuba 20.52). Under that definition 47.5 is right on three independent legs — the setting (ja-WP), the wiki's stated viewing order, the mid-match defect of 51.5 — and the licensor's listing (Vol.5 = 「TVスペシャル＋第48話～第56話」) corroborates.
- Does Toei's disc placement qualify as a licensor ORDER the way AoT's #3.5 did? Not on its own: it is a listing order, not an ordinal, and it does not name the slot. Rule 47.5 on the setting + the stated order (the protocol's own case) and cite the licensor as corroboration; do not write the entry as "the licensor's order", because a later reader will compare it with AoT's #3.5 and find less than the words claim.
- The note should say "parallel-world special" (finding 2): no slot is continuity-exact for this work; the seam is the honest one.
