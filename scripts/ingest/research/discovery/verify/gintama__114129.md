## The T28 question, decided on evidence

Is there an original share that would make this `mixed`? No. The film's job is the manga's ending — Lessons 699-704, the whole 完結編 — and every scene the sources record is the source's: the ten-minute Dragon Ball-parody prologue is a "previously on" (ANN: "a recap"; club-typhoon: 「これまでのあらすじ」; the licensor: the newcomer on-ramp; ウハル names the 687-698 beats it narrates), which under T28's recap clause counts for nothing in the standing and is rightly kept out of `chapters_adapted` (the haikyu precedent); the differences the sources name — every flashback redrawn, an added treatment of Takasugi's eye, the Utsuro fight's ending staged differently, cuts and reorders to fit 289 pages into ~90 minutes — are the faithfulness clause's "alterations, cuts, reorders and omissions of the source's own events"; and the one thing two sources call original (club-typhoon: 「唯一のアニメオリジナル要素」; ウハル: 「映画のオリジナル」) is the post-credit 3年Z組銀八先生 short — a gag at the tail, the size of a sliver, which is "NOTED, never weighed" (T28's sliver clause reads the same way from the original side: a fragment of original inside an adapted work does not flip the row). The recap boundary does not bite: no source describes a newly animated sequence of significant length carrying events the manga lacks — ja-WP's "アニメオリジナルの要素" and ANN's "new story elements" are, on every page that itemizes them, the prologue, the post-credit and expansions of adapted scenes.
