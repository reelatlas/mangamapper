## The position, decided on evidence

The KonoSuba precedent (21574, ruled 2026-09-05): the licensor's own slot (第1期 第11話 → 10.5) ordered the ledger and "the fandom's in-universe 14.5 is inference" went into the note. Here there is no licensor numbering (unlike Attack on Titan's #3.5), and the only source-side ORDER is the broadcast one — the fandom's P/N, en-WP's date-ordered specials list. The protocol's counter-clause ("a film set between S1 and S2 sits after the S1 finale even if it aired during S2") would apply if the work were CONTAINED by the gap. It is not: its coda animates the reunion and the Fish-Man Island departure (chs 598-602 = eps 517-522), its post-credits scene sits after the Straw Hats' return newspaper (ch 668's cover, post-Fish-Man Island), and Sakazuki wears the Fleet Admiral coat ch 650 / ep 570 reveals. At 516.5 a viewer meets three post-gap reveals before their episodes; at 658.5 nothing is ahead of the run (every referenced chapter ≤ 668 < ep 658's 726) except Sabo's faceless silhouette five episodes before ep 663 — which is what the broadcast itself did and the wiki already flags. The honest ledger position is therefore 658.5 with "in-universe placement: the two-year gap (516.5)" in the note — exactly what the verdict delivers. Sean rules; if he prefers the in-universe slot, 516.5 is free and the three caveats are verified.
