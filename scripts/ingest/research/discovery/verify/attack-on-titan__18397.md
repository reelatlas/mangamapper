## The T28 question, decided on evidence

Should a three-OAD bundle be judged per OAD, and is "one of three" a substantial source share? The ledger answers the first: I45 makes the AniList id the row and `RelatedWorkRuling` is keyed by `anilistId` with one standing — there is no sub-row, so the bundle takes one standing and the per-OAD facts live in the note (the verdict's flag 2 does exactly that; its "canon" label for #3.5 is the only part to correct). On the second, T28's test is "what is this work's job?", and the answer is two jobs in one entry: one OAD whose plot IS Isayama's special chapter — "a chapter, an omake, a side story … that IS the episode's plot all count", adapted in full, and the sole adaptation of it anywhere in the run — and two OADs that are entirely studio originals by the publisher's own description (完全新作アニメ). A whole chapter is significant by T28's own vocabulary ("significance not size"); two full originals are a substantial original share; neither is a sliver. Filler is excluded outright (a chapter is in it; overlap never proves filler and there is no overlap anyway); canon is excluded because two-thirds of the entry adapts nothing. `mixed`, `high`: two independent lineages (Kodansha + the licensor; en-WP + ja-WP + the wiki) name the same chapter, the same two originals and the same slot.
