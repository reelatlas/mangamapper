## Findings, ranked by damage

### 1. `standing: canon` — the evidence and the ruled T28 boundary yield MIXED (the same chapters)

**The claim.** "Under T28's faithfulness clause that is a divergence inside an adaptation whose job is unambiguously vol 5, not a story-carrying original plot, so the standing is canon, not mixed."

**What every source says about the material** (no disagreement anywhere): after Komekko's Railgun — the beat the novel ends the fight on — the film appends a wholly new act: Sylvia in the river of the dead, her resurrection as a Mage-Killer/Beldia/Hans amalgam, Wiz's Cursed Crystal Prison and Vanir's dolls, Darkness possessed by Vanir, Wiz collecting the villagers' magic, Kazuma stacking Blessings, professing love and being absorbed, the Yunyun + Megumin Light of Saber + Explosion finisher, Kazuma's remains, Eris, the Resurrection, the Guild welcome in Axel.
- fandom `Konosuba_Movie` §Differences: "Sylvia is fully killed when hit by the 'Railgun (TBD)' in the light novel, so the battles in the movie subsequent to that are all anime-original additions." Same section: "Red Prison is a totally anime-original remodel … occurs only in the movie."
- ja-WP 映画 この素晴らしい世界に祝福を！紅伝説 §シルビア (uncited, the editors' own comparison — independent of soneyu): 「原作ではこの段階で死亡したが、本作では後のオリジナルストーリーを挟んで死亡している。」
- fandom `Konosuba_Chapter_53` (manga, covers LN vol 5 ch 5-6): the Railgun "punches through Sylvia, and as everybody looks on in surprise at her sudden demise…" — then the rebuild and the Explosion upgrade.
- matcha-rabit: 「最大の違いは敵を倒すシーンですね！全てが違いました！… 原作にそんな話はないです！原作では「こめっこのレールガンで終わって」ます。… 映画化するということもあって物語のピークを「敵の撃破」に持っていきたい」; Infinite Zenith: "This is one of the deviations … the original text has the particle beam weapon as what permanently defeats Sylvia."

**Size.** No timestamped source exists (I looked: the Infinite Zenith long-form review gives none; the BD has no published chapter list I could find). By proportion of the fandom's plot summary the act is the last ~2.5 of ~15 paragraphs; by ja-WP's plot, half its final paragraph — consistent with the author's "roughly one sixth", i.e. 12-18 minutes of a 90-minute film, plus the Red Prison sequence (also original) before it.

**Why the canon reading does not hold under the rule as ruled.**
- T28's FAITHFULNESS CLAUSE covers "alterations, cuts, reorders and omissions of the source's OWN events" and carves out "a small studio-made beat inside an adapted chapter (a one-off scene, a sliver)". A resurrected villain, a second battle with two more generals' souls, the hero's death and resurrection, and four characters the novel does not put in the village are none of those four verbs and not a one-off scene. The author's own note calls it "the film's last act".
- T28's RECAP BOUNDARY (Sean, 2026-09-02, naruto-shippuden 388 — `episode-types.ts:897`): "a newly animated sequence of significant length is anime production that shapes the episode, and with scenes the source lacks it is original material however familiar its subject — it weighs toward filler; with a real canon share in the same episode → MIXED." That ruling weighed a ~15-minute newly animated sequence whose OUTCOME changed nothing; "same outcome as the novel" is therefore not a canon argument — it was exactly the shippuden-388 shape and Sean ruled it mixed.
- Batch 22 wave 2 (fullmetal-alchemist 13, Sean 2026-09-05): "Sean took the mixed reading over T28's faithfulness clause (the Marcoh thread is arc-launching, not connective)" — a studio act that carries the story's peak outweighs the clause. Same shape here: both JP reviewers say the film moved its dramatic peak INTO the original act.
- Nearest sibling shapes already ruled mixed: x 17 ("an anime-original climax" beside first-time source), toriko 144 ("half … canon, half … anime-original fight"). The canon-side witness, fist-of-the-north-star 130, is alterations of ch 178's own beats plus one small added beat — not an appended act.
- The wave's other canon films are not counter-precedents: Mugen Train's original opening (the Ubuyashiki graves) and Reze Arc's cold-open dream are slivers; the demon-slayer / chainsaw-man verdicts note them and stay canon. This film's original share is its climax.

**What the row should read.** `mixed` (high), chapters exactly as delivered (vol 4 Epilogue's closing scene + vol 5 ch 1-6 with the two holes), a note naming the post-Railgun act and Red Prison as the original share. The skip guide is standing + format (I45), so this is a rendered value — that is why it ranks first.

**What would settle it.** Only Sean's ruling on whether "significant length" is absolute (the shippuden scale, ~15 min → mixed) or proportional for feature films (~17% → a divergence). A timestamped breakdown (BD chapter markers, a scene-by-scene review) would sharpen the minutes but not the class: every source already agrees the act is original and that it is the climax.
