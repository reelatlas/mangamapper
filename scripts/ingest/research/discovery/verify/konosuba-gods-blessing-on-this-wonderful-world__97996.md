### 1. The verdict's own cited source carries a claim it does not address: fandom `Ran` prints "Light Novel: KonoSuba Volume 11 (Not Named)" — checked, it is NOT a printed home; filler stands

**The claim in the verdict.** "ja-WP files the OVA's lead guest ラン under アニメオリジナルキャラクター" — offered as a structural control for "nothing printed".

**What the cited source says.** The fandom's `Ran` page (in the verdict's source list) has `|Light Novel = Volume 11 (Not Named)`, `|Anime = KonoSuba OVA 2`, and Trivia: "She initially appears in the light novel as an unnamed newbie adventurer who admired Kazuma" (ref LN V11 C3); its History adds "Ran was one of the adventurers who agreed to feign admiration for the party … [Megumin's] letters" (LN V11 C3). A reader of the verdict would not learn that one of its sources half-contradicts the control.

**Timeline — the question is real, not the after-the-fact trap.** Vol 11 (大魔法使いの妹) shipped 2017-05-01 (Sneaker Bunko's series list); the OVA's bundle 2017-07-24. The novel precedes the OVA's release, so "does the OVA adapt vol 11 ch 3?" has to be answered, not dismissed.

**What vol 11 ch 3 actually contains.** The fandom's vol 11 `/Synopsis` page is EMPTY (headers only), so I read the chapter in the yuNS fan translation (cross-check tier): the Guild scene is Komekko being fussed over; Luna uses Komekko's sympathy ("don't you feel bad for spectre-san?") to push the reluctant adventurers into the unwanted "Lucy the Spectre" quest; Megumin admits her letters home were exaggerated; several adventurers vouch for the party, among them "the newbie female adventurer I had randomly chosen to dine and share adventuring wisdom with". Nothing of the OVA's story is in it: no Luna-hired fake fan, no handshake, no Titan Golem, no ancient ruins, no Doctor's diary, no Pretty Girl Golem, no Mitsurugi. The fandom's identification of that nameless extra as Ran is the wiki's inference (the text names nobody); ja-WP says the opposite (「OVAに登場したオリジナルキャラクター」) and both official copies say the episode was written new.

**Why the standing holds.** T28 asks what the work's job is: the OVA's job is an original golem / ruins / Doctor story with a fake-fan frame. What vol 11 ch 3 shares with it is a MOTIF (Luna's quest-pushing, admiration that forces quests, a newbie girl who vouches for Kazuma) — the same author reusing a comic device in the same months — not a scene and not a chapter. A motif is not an adaptation under the protocol; `chapters_adapted: []`, `filler` and `confidence: high` are right.

**What the verdict should have said** (one sentence, for the ruling's reason): "the fandom's Ran page tags her with an unnamed vol 11 ch 3 cameo — a wiki identification of a nameless extra in the Komekko / Lucy-the-Spectre Guild scene, which the OVA does not adapt." **What would settle it further:** the Japanese text of vol 11 ch 3 (the fan translation is cross-check only) — it would sharpen the wording, not the class.
