## The T28 question, decided on evidence

Is one standing for six tapes right, and is "three of six" a substantial source share? The ledger answers the first as it did for AoT: I45 makes the AniList id the row and `RelatedWorkRuling` is keyed by anilistId with one standing — no sub-row exists, so the bundle takes one standing and the per-tape facts live in the note (flag 1 does that; its Vol.3 label is the only part to correct). On the second, T28's test is "what is this work's job?", and the answer is two jobs in one entry: three tapes whose plots ARE Takahashi's arcs end to end — twelve chapters, each arc's only animation anywhere in the run — and three tapes that are studio originals on every lineage (ja-WP's OVAオリジナル marks; the wiki's `manga = None` and its "first / second / third OVA to not adapt a manga storyline"; furinkan's design-only credit; the 407-page sweep tying no chapter to them). Twelve whole chapters are significant by any reading ("significance not size"); three full originals are a substantial original share; neither is a sliver. Filler is excluded outright (overlap never proves filler, and there is no overlap — the chapters are first-time coverage); canon is excluded because half the entry adapts nothing. The AoT precedent (one special chapter beside two originals = mixed) is the weaker case and was ruled mixed; this one is mixed a fortiori. `mixed`, `high`: three independent lineages name the same chapters, the same three originals and the same seam.
