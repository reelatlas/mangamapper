Barely clinging to life inside a barrel after being swept through a violent sea whirlpool, the carefree Monkey D. Luffy suddenly finds himself aboard a ship under assault by ruthless pirates. Though he may appear to be a simple-minded teenager, Luffy is anything but weak. Possessing extraordinary combat ability, he is a pirate determined to claim the legendary One Piece and achieve the title of King of the Pirates.

Before his execution, the former King of the Pirates, Gol D. Roger, sent shockwaves across the world by revealing that his vast treasure was hidden somewhere and challenging anyone to find it. Since that moment, countless fearsome pirates have ventured into treacherous seas in search of the One Piece, many never to return. Despite starting his journey without a crew or even a proper ship, Luffy wields incredible powers and an indomitable will, making him both a dangerous opponent and a source of inspiration to others.

Facing every obstacle with an unwavering smile, Luffy gradually recruits unique and loyal allies to stand by his side. Together, they plunge headfirst into danger and discovery, sharing the thrills and hardships of a once-in-a-lifetime adventure.

Where to start One Piece manga after anime?
Where Does the Anime End? Where Should I Start Reading?

The anime ended at episode 1174, which corresponds to Chapter 1144 (p. 2-15) of the manga. The adaptation covers up to this point.

After watching the anime, to continue the story in the manga, start reading from Chapter 1145, which corresponds to Volume 113 (collecting chapters 1145 to 1155).

What One Piece fillers are worth watching?

While filler episodes don’t contribute to the main story or interfere with its plot, some are entertaining and worth watching. The best filler episodes include:

  1. Episodes 131–135 (Post-Alabasta): The Post-Alabasta arc is a set of transition episodes that delve into the dreams, pasts, and skills of the Straw Hat crew after the intense Alabasta saga. Although it’s filler, it stands out for its introspective tone and the small, character-focused stories for each crew member.
  2. Episodes 196–206 (G-8): The G-8 arc is considered one of the best fillers in anime, praised for its solid storytelling, character development, and unique perspective from the Marines’ point of view. After the Going Merry falls into the naval base Navarone, the soldiers gradually discover the Straw Hat Pirates’ infiltration.
  3. Episodes 220–224 (Ocean’s Dream): The Ocean’s Dream arc is a short and intriguing filler that uses memory loss as its central theme. It begins when a mysterious boy plays a horn shaped like a seahorse, causing the Straw Hats to forget much of their past.
  4. Episodes 780–782 (Marine Rookie): The Marine Rookie arc is a brief filler set between Zou and Whole Cake Island. It offers a fun break focused on Sanji’s “Rescue Team” searching for food. Starving after running out of supplies, Luffy and his group land on Fron Island—unaware that it houses a Marine base monitoring Big Mom’s movements.
