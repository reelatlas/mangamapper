Boruto: Naruto Next Generations (Definitive) Filler List

Updated on November 18, 2025

Boruto: Naruto Next Generations (Definitive) is an ongoing anime series that started in 2017. So far 293 episodes of Boruto: Naruto Next Generations (Definitive) have been aired. With a total of 102 reported filler episodes, Boruto: Naruto Next Generations (Definitive) has a moderate filler percentage of 35%.

This is the definitive filler list for Boruto: Naruto Next Generations.

Boruto Canon episodes are determined by whether they are adapted from the Boruto: Naruto Next Generations manga series, any of the Naruto spinoff manga, or any of the official novels. If episodes are not adapted from any of the manga series, they’re separated by "Filler" or "Anime Canon." "Anime Canon" episodes are non-adapted episodes that contain vital information that is referenced within the manga-adapted episodes.
